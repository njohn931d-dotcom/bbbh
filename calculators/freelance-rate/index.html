Freelance rate formula that doesn't leave you broke

Rate = (Desired Salary + Business Expenses + Taxes + Benefits) ÷ Billable Hours. Most new freelancers forget: you only bill ~50-60% of work hours. 40h week = 20-25 billable. Open source logic on GitHub.

Why freelancers charging $36/hr for $75k job go broke

$75k ÷ 2080 = $36/hr but you won't bill 2080. With 1000 billable hours (realistic first year), need $75/hr just for salary, $108 with benefits. See full freelance guide.

How does the cost of time calculator work?+

Divide the price of a purchase by your take-home hourly pay. A $150 purchase at $25 per hour costs 6 hours of work. For monthly income, we use 173.33 working hours per month; for annual income, 2,080 hours per year. These assume a 40-hour workweek over 52 weeks. If your schedule differs, enter your actual hourly take-home pay instead.

Should I use my salary before or after tax?+

Use after-tax pay—the money that actually reaches your bank account. For a more personal estimate, divide your take-home pay by the actual hours you work, including unpaid work time if you choose.

How are subscriptions and small savings calculated?+

Subscription check multiplies a monthly cost by 12, then divides the annual total by your hourly pay. Small habits multiplies a daily expense by 365 to show a possible yearly saving. Neither calculation includes investment returns, inflation, or price changes.

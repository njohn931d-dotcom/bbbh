Privacy Policy & Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Affiliate disclosure (FTC)

Affiliate Income Lab earns money from affiliate links. When you click certain links on this site and make a purchase, we may receive a commission. This costs you nothing extra — the price you pay is identical to buying directly.

In line with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's guidance on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255), and equivalent rules in other jurisdictions, we disclose this relationship clearly and before any affiliate link on every page where such links appear. We do not hide this in a footer or a terms page.

Affiliate relationships never change what we recommend. Programs are selected on earnings per click, cookie duration, tracking reliability and product quality — and we publish the reasoning so you can judge it yourself. We do not accept payment for placement or favourable coverage.

Earnings disclaimer

Every income figure on this site is an illustrative model or a published industry benchmark, not a promise. Calculators and projections use assumptions you choose and can change; real results depend on your niche, effort, skill, budget, competition and market conditions.

Affiliate marketing produces no guaranteed income, and many people who attempt it earn nothing at all. Nothing on this site is financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Consult a qualified professional before acting on anything you read here.

What data we collect

  • Calculator inputs: the income calculator runs entirely in your browser. Values you enter are never transmitted, stored or logged by us.
  • Email address: if you subscribe to our email list, we store your email address and the date you subscribed. We use it only to send you the content you asked for. Every message includes a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Analytics: we collect aggregated, non-identifying usage data — pages viewed, approximate country, referring source and device type — to understand which guides help people. We do not sell or share this data.
  • Server logs: our host records standard request logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for security and abuse prevention, retained for a limited period.

Cookies and tracking

Our own pages use minimal cookies. When you click an affiliate link, the merchant or affiliate network may set a cookie in your browser so that any purchase you make within that network's attribution window is credited to us. These cookies are set by the third party, not by us, and their duration varies by program — from 24 hours at some retailers to 90 days at some software vendors.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Doing so does not affect your ability to read anything on this site, and it does not change the price you pay for any product.

Third parties we link to

We link to merchants, affiliate networks and other publishers. Once you follow a link, that third party's own privacy policy and terms govern what happens to your data. We have no control over, and accept no responsibility for, the practices of external sites.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under GDPR in the EU and UK, and CCPA/CPRA in California — you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete personal data we hold about you, and to object to certain processing. Our email list is the only place we hold personal data, and you can unsubscribe or request deletion at any time.

Children

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We update this page when our practices change, and we update the date at the top when we do. Material changes will be announced on the site.

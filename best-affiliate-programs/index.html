27 Best Affiliate Programs Compared by Payout Tier (2026)

RC By Riley Chen Updated 26 September 2026 12 min read

Quick answer: the best affiliate programs in 2026 are not the ones with the biggest brand names — they are the ones with the highest earnings per click. Recurring software programs paying 20–40% monthly (hosting, email, CRM, AI tools) win for most publishers, high-ticket B2B and finance lead-gen programmes pay the most per visitor, and Amazon Associates remains the best testing ground despite paying only 1–10%. Join three to six programs across different payout tiers rather than a dozen in one.

Before you apply

Commission rates, cookie windows and program terms change frequently — often quarterly, and often without notice. The figures below are typical published rates at the time of writing and are grouped in ranges deliberately. Always confirm current terms on the vendor's own affiliate page before you build content around a program.

How we rank affiliate programs

We score every program on five criteria, weighted by how much they affect a real publisher's income:

  1. Earnings per click (heaviest weight). Commission value × realistic conversion rate. A $500 payout that converts at 0.5% beats a $5 payout that converts at 3%.
  2. Recurring vs one-off. Recurring revenue compounds; a one-off payment does not. A 20% recurring program almost always out-earns a 50% one-time program within a year.
  3. Cookie duration and attribution. 24 hours is harsh; 30 days is standard; 90 days is a genuine advantage for considered purchases.
  4. Approval difficulty and reliability. Consistent tracking, on-time payouts, and no history of retroactive commission cuts.
  5. Product quality. You cannot build a durable audience promoting products that churn or disappoint.

Tier 1: Recurring software and hosting — the best risk-adjusted bet

These programs pay a share of every monthly payment for as long as the customer stays. If you only ever build one income stream with affiliate marketing, build it on recurring software. The maths is the reason: 100 customers referred at $29/month recurring is roughly $2,900 every month from work you already finished.

Why recurring beats a bigger one-off number

A $300 one-off commission looks better than $29/month — until you do the year. If the customer stays 14 months, the recurring program paid $406 from a single click and keeps paying. Always model the expected customer lifetime, not the first payment.

Application checklist: get approved the first time

  1. Have a real property. A site with 20+ genuinely useful pages, or a channel with an engaged audience. Empty sites are rejected, and reapplying too often can count against you.
  2. Publish a privacy policy and a clear affiliate disclosure page. Most programs (and most jurisdictions) require both. Missing them is a common automated rejection reason.
  3. Match the vertical. Apply to programs that fit your existing content. A general finance blog applying to a developer tool will be declined.
  4. Write a one-paragraph pitch. State your niche, monthly traffic or subscribers, how you will promote the product, and where the link will live. This alone lifts approval odds significantly.
  5. Start with easier networks. Marketplaces and mid-tier networks approve broad audiences; direct enterprise programs usually want a track record. Apply to the easy ones first, then trade up with data.
  6. Track everything from day one. Use consistent sub-IDs per page so you know which content produces revenue. Without this you are guessing, and guessing is expensive.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best affiliate program for beginners?

Amazon Associates is the easiest to be approved for and converts extremely well, which makes it the best testing ground even at 1–10%. For income you can actually live on, pair it with a recurring software program paying 20–40% every month — that combination needs far less traffic for the same revenue.

Which affiliate programs pay recurring commissions?

Most subscription software, web hosting, website builders, email marketing platforms, CRM tools and AI services pay recurring commissions — typically 20–40% of each monthly payment for as long as the customer stays subscribed. Recurring programs are almost always worth more than a one-off commission at the same headline percentage.

What is a good affiliate commission rate?

It depends entirely on the product price. Physical retail runs 1–10%, digital products and courses 30–50%, SaaS 20–40% recurring, and lead generation $20–$500 per approved application. Judge a program by expected earnings per click, not the headline percentage — 5% of a $1,000 product beats 50% of a $30 course.

How long should an affiliate cookie last?

Thirty days is the practical minimum and 90 days is strong. Amazon uses a 24-hour cookie, which is exactly why Amazon income depends on high-converting, high-volume traffic. For considered purchases with long research cycles, moving from a 24-hour to a 90-day cookie can double your effective conversion rate.

Can you join affiliate programs with no website?

Some — many software and marketplace programs accept a YouTube channel, newsletter or social audience as your promotional property. Amazon requires a website, app or channel with demonstrable traffic. If you are rejected, build a small site or public channel first and reapply; most networks will reconsider.

How many affiliate programs should one site join?

Three to six active programs per site is the sweet spot: one or two high-volume retail programs, at least one recurring software program, and one high-ticket or lead-generation offer. More than that fragments your data and makes it hard to know which relationships are actually earning.

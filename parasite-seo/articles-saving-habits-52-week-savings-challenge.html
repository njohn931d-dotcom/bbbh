The 52-Week Savings Challenge: Full Table and Five Variations

The complete 52-week money challenge table, five variations including the reverse and bi-weekly versions, and totals from $689 to $6,890.

The 52-week challenge works like this: in week 1 you save $1, week 2 you save $2, and so on until week 52, when you save $52. The total is $1,378 — the sum of 1 to 52.

The appeal is the shape: tiny amounts early, bigger amounts later, when the habit has formed.

At the halfway point (week 26) you have saved $351 — 25% of the total. The second half carries 75% of the work, which is exactly when December arrives.

Five variations

VariationHow it worksYear total
Classic$1 in week 1 up to $52 in week 52$1,378

The reverse version front-loads the pain while motivation is high and gets easier as the year goes on — better if you expect a tight December. The random draw version lets you pick a small number in an expensive week, which is why many people finish it.

Making it survive December

The classic challenge asks for $50, $51 and $52 in the three most expensive weeks of the year. Three fixes:

  1. Run it in reverse so December is cheap.
  2. Shift the calendar to start in January and bank the big weeks in a quiet month by reordering which week gets which amount.
  3. Save the December weeks in advance during a cheaper month, then "spend" those savings into the challenge when the weeks arrive.

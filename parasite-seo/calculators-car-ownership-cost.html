GOOD QUESTIONS

Answers, written down.

What is the cheapest car to own?

A used car that is reliable and cheap to insure, rather than the cheapest car to buy. Purchase price is a poor predictor of ownership cost.

How much should I budget per mile for a car?

A common planning figure is 60 to 70 cents a mile for an average used car, higher for insurance-heavy or urban drivers. Run your own numbers for your mileage.

Does driving less really change the cost per mile?

Yes. Fixed costs — insurance, registration, depreciation — do not shrink when you drive less, so cost per mile rises quickly.

Your Real Hourly Wage: Counting Commute, Overtime and Unpaid Work

Work out what you actually earn per hour once commute, preparation time, unpaid overtime and work costs are included, with tables and the break-even test.

Your real hourly wage is your take-home pay divided by every hour the job costs you — not just the hours on your contract. Real hourly wage = (take-home pay − work costs) ÷ total hours given to work.

A $60,000 salary at 40 contracted hours is $28.85 an hour gross. Add a 45-minute daily commute, 30 minutes of getting ready and five hours of unpaid overtime a week, and the same job is closer to $19 an hour.

The formula, step by step

  1. Annual take-home pay (after tax and deductions)
  2. Minus work costs: commuting, parking, work clothes, childcare, meals bought at work
  3. Equals net pay for the job
  4. Divided by total hours: contracted hours + commute + preparation + unpaid overtime + work admin at home

The advertised rate was $28.85 gross. The real rate is $14.88 — and that is the figure to use when deciding whether a purchase is worth the hours.

HOW THESE NUMBERS ARE CALCULATED

Every figure on this page is arithmetic from stated assumptions: take-home pay, an 8-hour working day and 2,080 working hours a year (40 hours × 52 weeks), unless stated otherwise. Rates, taxes, fees and prices change, so treat the tables as a way to see the shape of a decision rather than a quote. Worth is a perspective tool, not financial advice.

After-tax formula

Take-home = Gross - Federal - State - FICA (7.65%) - Other. 2025 federal brackets 10-37%. Our open-source calculator on GitHub uses simple estimate - consult CPA for exact. But good for hourly perspective.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Answers, written down.

Should I budget on gross or take-home?

Take-home, always. Budgeting on gross is the single most common reason people run short at the end of the month.

How is take-home different from net pay?

They are the same thing. Net pay, take-home and take-home pay all mean the amount after tax and other deductions.

What is a good take-home to live on in the US?

It depends entirely on where you live and your circumstances. A rough anchor: aim to keep housing under 30% of take-home and save 15–20% before discretionary spending.

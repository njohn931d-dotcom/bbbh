$20 an Hour Is How Much a Year? (Gross, Take-Home and Monthly)

$20 an hour is $41,600 a year gross at 2,080 hours. See monthly, weekly and daily figures, take-home estimates by tax rate, and what changes the total.

At 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, $20 an hour is $41,600 a year before tax. That is $3,466.67 a month, $1,600 every two weeks, $800 a week and $160 a working day.

What changes the $41,600

SituationAnnual effect
Unpaid vacation (2 weeks)2,000 hours → $40,000
Overtime (4 h/week at 1.5×)+$6,240 → $47,840
Part-time at 30 h/week1,560 hours → $31,200
52 weeks including paid vacation$41,600 (fewer hours worked)
Raised to $22 mid-year~$43,680 in the following year

Unpaid leave is the most common surprise: at 40 hours a week with two weeks unpaid, the year is 2,000 working hours, not 2,080, and the annual figure drops by $1,600.

HOW THESE NUMBERS ARE CALCULATED

Every figure on this page is arithmetic from stated assumptions: take-home pay, an 8-hour working day and 2,080 working hours a year (40 hours × 52 weeks), unless stated otherwise. Rates, taxes, fees and prices change, so treat the tables as a way to see the shape of a decision rather than a quote. Worth is a perspective tool, not financial advice.

Amazon Affiliate Commission Rates 2026: Full Category Table

RC By Riley Chen, Founder & Lead Researcher Updated 26 September 2026 9 min read

Quick answer: Amazon Associates pays roughly 1% to 10% of the item price, varying by product category — not a single flat rate. The cookie lasts only 24 hours, and you earn on everything in the qualifying cart, not just the item you linked. At a typical $9 average commission you need about 92,000 monthly visitors to earn $1,000/month, which is why Amazon is best used to learn what your audience buys, then paired with a recurring or high-ticket program for actual margin.

Read this before using the table below

Amazon changes commission rates by category, and has done so repeatedly with little notice — sometimes cutting a category from 8% to 3% overnight. The figures below are representative bands based on Amazon's published category structure, not a live feed. Always confirm current rates in your own Associates dashboard before building a content strategy around any single category.

Key takeaways

  • Rates are per category, not flat. The spread between the cheapest and richest category is roughly 10×.
  • The 24-hour cookie is the real constraint — not the commission rate. It decides which content can work at all.
  • You earn on the whole cart within 24 hours, including items you never linked.
  • ~92,000 visitors/month for $1,000 at typical rates. Treat Amazon as a testing ground, not a destination.
  • Add a recurring program in week one. Same traffic, roughly 5–10× the revenue.

The 24-hour cookie matters more than the rate

Most publishers fixate on the commission percentage and ignore the cookie window. That is backwards, because the cookie decides whether your content can convert at all.

Same product, same price. The attribution window changes everything.
ScenarioCookieConversion rateCommission on a $300 purchase
Impulse buy, price compared24 hours4.0%$12 (at 4%)
Considered purchase, 2-week decision24 hours0.6%$12 (at 4%)
Same considered purchase via a 90-day network90 days3.2%$45 (at 15%)

The middle row is the trap. If you write deeply researched content for an expensive, considered purchase — exactly the content that earns trust — the 24-hour window means most of your readers buy after your cookie has expired. You did the work and a different network got paid.

The fix is not to avoid Amazon; it is to match the content type to the cookie:

  • Amazon suits: impulse purchases, low price points, “buy now” intent, accessories, gifting, and categories where the buyer already knows the model number they want.
  • Amazon does not suit: $1,000+ considered purchases, B2B software, anything with a research cycle measured in weeks — those belong with a 30–90 day network.

The strategy this table implies

Use Amazon to discover what your audience actually buys — its conversion data is the best in the industry — then move that same traffic to a program that pays more. Publishers who keep only Amazon and keep publishing more content are solving the wrong problem: they need a better payout, not more visitors. See the payout tiers here or recurring programs here.

How to make Amazon work anyway

  1. Target impulse-intent keywords. “[Product] deal”, “[product] price”, “cheap [product]” — buyers ready to purchase in one session, where 24 hours is plenty.
  2. Push the whole cart. A well-timed “what else you'll need with this” section increases basket size, and you earn on every qualifying item, not just the linked product.
  3. Use Subscribe & Save angles. Consumables and pet supplies generate repeat orders, which partially offsets the short cookie.
  4. Add a second network with a longer window. Put the deep-research pages on a 30–90 day network and keep Amazon for the quick-purchase pages. Same site, higher blended revenue.
  5. Layer an email list. A reader who does not buy inside 24 hours is not a lost cause — if you can reach them again when they are ready.

Amazon Associates approval and rules

  • You need a real property. A website, app or channel with demonstrable traffic and original content. Amazon reviews it manually.
  • Disclose clearly. Amazon's operating agreement requires a specific disclosure statement, and the FTC requires it above the link — not in your footer.
  • Make a sale within 180 days. Accounts with no qualifying sales in that window are closed, and you must reapply.
  • Friends, family and your own orders do not count. Commission is void on orders from your household or anyone you have a personal relationship with.
  • Rate changes apply to future sales. Amazon can change category rates at any time, including retroactively for items not yet shipped. Never build a business on one category.

FAQ

How much does Amazon Associates pay per sale?

A percentage of item price that varies by category — generally between about 1% and 10%, with some digital categories higher. It is not a single flat rate. Rates are set by Amazon and have changed repeatedly with little notice, so confirm current figures in your Associates dashboard before committing to a category.

Why is the Amazon cookie only 24 hours?

Amazon credits you when a customer adds an eligible item to their cart within 24 hours of your click or buys immediately. That is much shorter than the 30–90 day windows independent networks offer, which is decisive for considered purchases. It means Amazon rewards content that drives immediate buying intent.

How much traffic for $1,000 a month?

At a $9 average commission, a 4% click-through rate and a 3% conversion rate, revenue is about $10.80 per 1,000 visitors — so roughly 92,000 monthly visitors. That is why most publishers use Amazon to validate demand, then shift the same traffic to a higher-paying program.

Do you earn commission on the whole cart?

Yes, within the 24-hour window. If a customer clicks your link, adds eligible items and checks out in time, you earn on the qualifying items in that order — not only the product you linked to. This halo effect is a genuine Amazon advantage over single-product programs.

What is the approval process?

Apply with a website, app or channel that has demonstrable traffic and original content. Amazon reviews it manually and expects a visible affiliate disclosure and privacy policy. You must make qualifying sales within 180 days or the account closes. Your own orders and those of people you know do not count.

What are the main alternatives?

Independent retail networks pay 3–15% with 30–60 day cookies. For substantially more income per visitor, publishers move to recurring software (20–40% monthly), high-ticket B2B, or finance and insurance cost-per-lead.

About Affiliate Income Lab

We publish the affiliate marketing research we wanted when we started — keyword data, program economics and honest numbers. No courses to sell, no gurus, no income claims.

What this site is

Affiliate Income Lab is an independent research site about earning money with affiliate marketing. Every guide starts from the same premise: affiliate income is a four-variable equation, not a secret. Traffic × click-through rate × conversion rate × average commission. If you understand which variable you are actually moving, you stop wasting months on tactics that cannot pay.

Who writes it

RC

Riley Chen — Founder & Lead Researcher

Riley builds and audits affiliate content sites, and writes about keyword selection, program economics and affiliate site operations. The guides here document what the data shows, including the parts that are unflattering — like the fact that roughly half of people who start affiliate marketing never earn a single commission.

How we research

  • Keyword volumes are directional aggregates of publicly available keyword-tool estimates. We deliberately present them as ranges rather than false precision, because different tools disagree by wide margins.
  • Commission structures come from publicly published affiliate program terms. Ranges are used because vendors change rates frequently and often without notice.
  • Income projections are modelled from published industry benchmarks — typical click-through rates, merchant conversion rates and average order values. They are illustrations, never promises.
  • Recommendations are based on earnings per click, cookie duration and program reliability, not on which vendor pays the highest headline rate or offers us the best deal.
Our editorial rule

If we cannot explain why a recommendation makes the reader money, we do not publish it. That has cost us revenue — we have turned down programs with generous payouts that we did not believe in — and it is the reason the numbers here are worth reading.

How we make money

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you click one and buy, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We also publish the reasoning behind every program we recommend, so you can judge the advice on its merits rather than on whether we were paid. We do not accept payment for placement, and we do not run sponsored articles presented as editorial.

Corrections

If any figure here is out of date or wrong, we want to fix it. Affiliate program terms change constantly, and a correct chart beats a popular one. Report an error via the contact address published on our privacy page.

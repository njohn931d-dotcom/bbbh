## Per hour lived
Home cost/hr = (Mortgage+Tax+Insurance+Maintenance) ÷ hours at home.

$2,000 rent vs $2,900 own ($500k home): rent $5/hr, buy $7.25/hr minus $1.50 equity = $5.75/hr true.

Add 10h/mo maintenance time.

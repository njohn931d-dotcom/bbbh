Who writes RevenueKit, and how the numbers get made

RevenueKit is an independent publisher and software studio with one question: which ways of generating income online actually pay, at what cost, and how fast. We answer it with public data, first-hand revenue history and conservative ranges — then sell the tools that make the answers executable.

How we score a method

Startup cost
Cash outlay before the first sale, including tools and hosting realistically required.
Time to first dollar
Median weeks from committed start to first payment, part-time effort (8–15 h/week).
Realistic month, year one
The interquartile range we observe for committed part-time sellers — not the top screenshot.
Maintenance hours
Monthly hours once live: support, updates, marketing. This is the number “passive” advice hides.
Ceiling
Whether revenue scales independently of your hours (5) or is strictly time-bound (1).

Research & testing method

  1. Public data first. Platform fee pages, marketplace payout disclosures and published creator-economy research (Precedence Research, Goldman Sachs, platform economic reports) set the outer bounds of every range.
  2. First-hand second. Our own eleven-product revenue history since 2019 calibrates the middle of the range — including the launches that failed, which teach more than the ones that worked.
  3. Community-documented third. Indie revenue reports and build logs cross-check both; where all three disagree we publish the conservative end and say so in-line.
  4. Quarterly re-verification. Fees, prices and ranges are re-checked at least every quarter; each guide carries published and last-verified dates.

We do not accept payment for placement, we do not sell sponsored posts, and we do not run display ads. Revenue comes from our own kits and — where labelled — affiliate relationships that never change a ranking.

Affiliate & commercial disclosure

Some guides mention platforms or tools that operate affiliate programmes. Where a link could earn us a commission, it is labelled in-line. More importantly: we sell competing kits ourselves, so our incentive is to recommend what actually fits — a reader who buys the wrong $149 kit refunds it and never returns, which is the worst outcome available to us. Every comparison table on this site includes free and competitor options for exactly this reason.

Privacy, briefly

No third-party analytics, no ad networks, no fingerprinting. This site sets one first-party preference (your light/dark theme choice) in local storage and nothing else. Newsletter addresses are used only to send the newsletter and are never sold, shared or enriched. Email us any time for a copy or deletion of your data.

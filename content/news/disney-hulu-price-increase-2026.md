Disney raised prices on most Disney+ and Hulu plans on September 23, 2026. According to [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/09/disney-hikes-price-disney-hulu-streaming-plans-1237111325/), it is the company's fourth increase in four years, and the biggest jump landed on the ad-free Premium plans: Disney+ Premium and Hulu Premium each went from $18.99 to $21.49 a month, up $2.50 or 13%. The ad-supported plans rose 50 cents to $12.49.

## The new prices, monthly and yearly

| Plan | Before | Now | Change | Per year now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Disney+ with ads | $11.99 | $12.49 | +$0.50 | $149.88 |
| Hulu with ads | $11.99 | $12.49 | +$0.50 | $149.88 |
| Disney+ Premium (no ads) | $18.99 | $21.49 | +$2.50 | $257.88 |
| Hulu Premium (no ads) | $18.99 | $21.49 | +$2.50 | $257.88 |
| Disney+ and Hulu bundle, no ads | $19.99 | $21.99 | +$2.00 | $263.88 |
| Disney+ and Hulu bundle, with ads | $12.99 | $12.99 | no change | $155.88 |

Deadline also reports that the Disney+, Hulu and ESPN bundle with ads rose $2 to $21.99 a month and the ad-free Premium Select bundle rose $3 to $32.99, while the Unlimited bundles stayed at $35.99 with ads and $44.99 without.

## The bundle is the obvious move

Two Premium plans bought separately now cost $42.98 a month, or $515.76 a year. The ad-free Disney+ and Hulu bundle costs $21.99 a month, or $263.88 a year. Bundling saves $20.99 a month, $251.88 a year, for the same two services. Variety, as reported by [WFSB](https://www.wfsb.com/2026/09/23/disney-hulu-are-raising-streaming-prices-again/), says the pricing is meant to push customers toward bundles.

## How to keep the bill down

- **List every subscription** and the last time you used it. The [subscription cost calculator](/calculators/subscription-cost/) shows each one as a yearly total and in hours of work.
- **Rotate.** Keep one or two services at a time and cancel the rest between shows you actually want to watch.
- **Price the no-ads upgrade.** The gap between Disney+ with ads and Disney+ Premium is $9.00 a month, $108 a year. Decide whether that is worth it to you.
- **Compare the bundle** before your next bill if you use both Disney+ and Hulu.

Rockstar confirmed the price of Grand Theft Auto VI on June 24, 2026: **$79.99** for the standard edition and **$99.99** for the Ultimate Edition, which adds exclusive cosmetic bonuses, according to [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-price-confirmed-ultimate-edition-rockstar-games/). The game launches on November 19, 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, a date Take-Two reaffirmed in its [earnings report](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/gta-6-is-locked-in-for-november-19-2026-according-to-take-two-interactive-despite-delay-rumors-and-marketing-starts-soon).

## The price in context

Polygon notes that standard big-budget releases have been settling around $70 this console generation. At $79.99, GTA VI is $10 (about 14%) above that mark, and the Ultimate Edition costs another $20 on top.

## Hours of work for the standard edition

::hours 79.99 | GTA VI standard edition

At the federal minimum wage of $7.25, the standard edition costs about 12 hours of work after tax, a day and a half. At $25 an hour it is under 4 hours.

## The Ultimate Edition

::hours 99.99 | GTA VI Ultimate Edition

The extra $20 for the Ultimate Edition is roughly an hour and a half of work at $15 an hour, or about an hour at $25.

## Cost per hour of play

The useful number for a game is the price per hour you actually play, which works like cost per wear for clothes.

| Hours played | Standard ($79.99) | Ultimate ($99.99) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 | $8.00 an hour | $10.00 an hour |
| 25 | $3.20 an hour | $4.00 an hour |
| 50 | $1.60 an hour | $2.00 an hour |
| 100 | $0.80 an hour | $1.00 an hour |

For comparison, one movie ticket at the $16.30 U.S. average for a two-hour film works out to $8.15 an hour. The comparison does not say which is better. It says a long game is cheap per hour if you play it and expensive if it sits unfinished.

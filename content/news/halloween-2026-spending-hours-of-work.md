The National Retail Federation expects Halloween spending to reach **$13.5 billion** in 2026, or **$115.14 per person** on average. The figures come from a survey of 7,889 U.S. consumers conducted September 1 to 9 by Prosper Insights & Analytics, reported in the [NRF's Halloween data](https://nrf.com/blog/halloween-shoppers-find-ways-to-stretch-their-budgets). Halloween falls on Saturday, October 31, 2026.

## The survey in five numbers

- **74%** of consumers plan to celebrate.
- **$115.14** is the average planned spend per person.
- **49%** started shopping in September or earlier.
- **79%** expect tariffs to push prices higher.
- **34%** say they look for items they can reuse.

## $115.14 in hours of work

::hours 115.14 | The average Halloween budget

At $15 an hour the average budget is about 9 hours of work after tax. At $25 an hour it is about 5 hours.

## A household of four

The NRF figure is per person who celebrates. If everyone in a household of four spent the average, the total would be $460.56.

::hours 460.56 | A household of four spending the average

That is about 35 hours of after-tax work at $15 an hour, and about 22 hours at $25 an hour.

## Spending less without skipping it

- **Reuse.** The NRF found reuse is one of the most common ways shoppers plan to cope with higher prices. A costume worn three times costs a third as much per wear.
- **Set the number first.** Write down the total before the first store visit, then track it against the budget.
- **Buy what gets used.** Candy that gets eaten and decorations stored for next year beat a costume worn once.
- **Wait a day.** The [24-hour rule](/guides/24-hour-rule/) is the cheapest filter for seasonal impulse buys.
- **See the small stuff add up.** The [daily savings calculator](/calculators/daily-savings/) shows what small amounts become over a year.

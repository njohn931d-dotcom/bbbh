Four big films open between October 16 and December 18, 2026. The average U.S. movie ticket costs **$16.30** this year, and a date night for two with a large popcorn averages **$54.08**, according to [CableTV.com's 2026 survey](https://www.cabletv.com/entertainment/cost-of-a-movie-ticket). Here is what that is in hours of work.

## A night out in hours of work

::hours 54.08 | Two tickets and a large popcorn

At $25 an hour, a night out for two is about 2.6 hours of work after tax. At $15 an hour it is about 4.1 hours.

## Where the price varies

The survey found the average ticket ranges from $12.73 in Arizona to $25.24 in New York, and a date night from $44.73 in Tennessee to $73.39 in New York. At the top end, [The Independent reported](https://www.the-independent.com/us/money/movie-tickets-price-increase-expensive-b2971595.html) that tickets for a recent Dune: Part Three screening reached $50, although $50 tickets remain uncommon. The same article cites a Pew Research Center analysis showing ticket sales in the U.S. and Canada fell 37% between 2019 and 2025.

## Home or theater

One average date night costs about as much as 2.7 months of Netflix Standard at $19.99 a month. The two are not the same experience, but the comparison is a useful gut check when a film is a maybe rather than a must.

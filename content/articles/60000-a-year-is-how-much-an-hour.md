Divide $60,000 by 2,080 working hours (40 a week for 52 weeks) and you get **$28.85 an hour** before tax. After tax at a 25% effective rate, that is about $21.64 an hour of money you actually receive.

## The conversion table

| Period | Gross amount | Calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Per year | $60,000 | — |
| Per month | $5,000 | 60,000 ÷ 12 |
| Semi-monthly | $2,500 | 60,000 ÷ 24 |
| Every two weeks | $2,307.69 | 60,000 ÷ 26 |
| Per week | $1,153.85 | 60,000 ÷ 52 |
| Per day (8 h) | $230.77 | 28.85 × 8 |
| Per hour | $28.85 | 60,000 ÷ 2,080 |

## The hourly rate depends on hours worked

The 2,080-hour divisor assumes a 40-hour week with no unpaid leave. Change the hours and the hourly figure changes:

| Weekly hours | Annual hours | Hourly rate on $60,000 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 35 | 1,820 | $32.97 |
| 37.5 | 1,950 | $30.77 |
| 40 | 2,080 | $28.85 |
| 45 | 2,340 | $25.64 |
| 50 | 2,600 | $23.08 |
| 55 | 2,860 | $20.98 |
| 60 | 3,120 | $19.23 |

At 50 hours a week, a $60,000 salary pays less per hour than a $52,000 salary at 40 hours ($25.00). Hours worked matter as much as the number on the contract.

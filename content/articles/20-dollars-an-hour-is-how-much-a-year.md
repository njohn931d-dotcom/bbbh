At 40 hours a week for 52 weeks, **$20 an hour is $41,600 a year** before tax. That is $3,466.67 a month, $1,600 every two weeks, $800 a week and $160 a working day.

## The full conversion table

| Period | Gross amount | Calculation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Per hour | $20.00 | — |
| Per day (8 h) | $160.00 | 20 × 8 |
| Per week (40 h) | $800.00 | 20 × 40 |
| Every two weeks | $1,600.00 | 20 × 80 |
| Semi-monthly | $1,733.33 | 41,600 ÷ 24 |
| Per month | $3,466.67 | 41,600 ÷ 12 |
| Per year | $41,600.00 | 20 × 2,080 |

## What $20 an hour looks like after tax

Take-home depends on your tax system, deductions and allowances, so these are illustrations rather than calculations of what you will receive:

| Effective tax + deductions | Take-home a year | Take-home a month | Effective hourly rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10% | $37,440 | $3,120 | $18.00 |
| 20% | $33,280 | $2,773 | $16.00 |
| 25% | $31,200 | $2,600 | $15.00 |
| 30% | $29,120 | $2,427 | $14.00 |
| 35% | $27,040 | $2,253 | $13.00 |

The "effective hourly rate" column is the number to use when converting a purchase into hours of work, because it is the money you actually receive.

## What changes the $41,600

| Situation | Annual effect |
| --- | --- |
| Unpaid vacation (2 weeks) | 2,000 hours → $40,000 |
| Overtime (4 h/week at 1.5×) | +$6,240 → $47,840 |
| Part-time at 30 h/week | 1,560 hours → $31,200 |
| 52 weeks including paid vacation | $41,600 (fewer hours worked) |
| Raised to $22 mid-year | ~$43,680 in the following year |

Unpaid leave is the most common surprise: at 40 hours a week with two weeks unpaid, the year is 2,000 working hours, not 2,080, and the annual figure drops by $1,600.

A $2.50 raise adds $5,200 a year — $433 a month. Working out the annual figure of a raise is the fastest way to judge whether a job change is worth it.

## Converting it back: your real hourly rate

$20 an hour is the paid rate. Your *real* rate divides pay by every hour the job costs you, including commute, unpaid overtime and getting ready:

| Weekly hours actually spent | Real hourly rate at $800/week |
| --- | --- |
| 40 | $20.00 |
| 45 | $17.78 |
| 50 | $16.00 |
| 55 | $14.55 |
| 60 | $13.33 |

See [Your Real Hourly Wage](/articles/pay-and-rates/your-real-hourly-wage/) for the full method.

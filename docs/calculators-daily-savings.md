## What is Daily Savings Calculator?

 Turn a daily habit into a yearly saving   Yearly savings = daily amount × 365.  Setting aside $5 each day adds up to $1,825 over a 365-day year. This is money set aside, not an investment forecast.  How much could you save in a year?     Daily amount  Over 30 days  Over 365 days      $1  $30  $365    $5  $150  $1,825    $10  $300  $3,650     What about coffee only on weekdays?  The calculator assumes a daily habit. If you skip a $5 purchase five times a week for 52 weeks, the result is $1,300—not $1,825. For twice a week, it is $520. Use the schedule that matches your life.  Make the change sustainable  Pick a purchase you will not miss, and move the amount into a separate savings pot. Cutting a purchase does not increase savings if the money is simply spent elsewhere. Keep the things that give you real value.

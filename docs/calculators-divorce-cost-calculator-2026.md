## What divorce costs in 2026

Cost = attorney hours × hourly rate + filing fees + expert fees. Uncontested filings with online forms run $300–$1,500. Mediation typically $3,000–$8,000. Litigated cases with children or property disputes average $15,000–$30,000 per spouse and can pass $100,000.

| Route | Typical cost per spouse | Hours at $30/hr |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DIY uncontested | $500 | 17 h |
| Mediated | $6,000 | 200 h |
| Collaborative | $12,000 | 400 h |
| Litigated with children | $25,000 | 833 h |

## In hours of work

A $20,000 litigated divorce at $30 per hour after tax is 667 hours, four months of full-time work. Attorneys bill $250–$500 per hour, so each hour you argue costs you 8–17 hours of your own labor.

## Lowering the bill

Agree on as much as possible before involving lawyers, use mediation for the rest, and keep communication in writing to reduce billable back-and-forth. Every avoided attorney hour is roughly a working day saved.

## FAQ

**How much does a divorce cost in 2026?**

From a few hundred dollars for an uncontested filing to $15,000–$30,000 per spouse for a contested case.

**What makes divorce expensive?**

Disputes over custody, property, or support that require attorney time, expert valuations, and court appearances.

**How do I convert legal fees to hours of work?**

Divide the total fee by your after-tax hourly pay.

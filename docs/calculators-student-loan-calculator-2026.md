## Payment in hours

Hours per month = loan payment ÷ take-home hourly pay. A $380 payment at $24 per hour is 15.8 hours a month, about two working days. Over a 10-year standard plan that is 1,900 hours in total.

## 2026 federal rates

Undergraduate direct loans for 2025–26 carry 6.39% interest; graduate loans 7.94%; PLUS loans 8.94%. A $30,000 balance at 6.39% over 10 years costs $338 a month and $10,600 in interest.

## Faster payoff

Adding $100 a month to the $338 payment clears the loan in about 7.3 years and saves roughly $3,000 of interest, which is 125 hours of work returned to you.

## FAQ

**How much of my paycheck should go to student loans?**

Under the standard plan most borrowers pay 8–12% of take-home pay; income-driven plans cap payments at 10% of discretionary income.

**How is a student loan payment calculated?**

Same amortization formula as any fixed loan: balance × monthly rate ÷ (1 − (1 + monthly rate)^−months).

**Does paying extra help?**

Yes. Extra payments reduce principal directly, cutting both the payoff time and total interest.

## 1. The 4% Safe Withdrawal Rate

The 4% Rule, established by the Trinity Study (Cooley, Hubbard, and Walz), found that a diversified portfolio of 50% stocks and 50% bonds had a 95% probability of surviving a 30-year retirement without depletion when withdrawing 4% in year one, adjusted for inflation annually thereafter.

For longer horizons (40–50+ years typical of early retirees), conservative practitioners utilize a **3.25% to 3.5% safe withdrawal rate (SWR)**.

## 4. Types of FIRE

- **Lean FIRE:** Target annual spend under $40,000/year (strict frugality, geo-arbitrage, minimal fixed commitments). Portfolio: $750k–$1M.
- **Traditional FIRE:** Matches standard middle-class lifestyle ($50k–$90k/year). Portfolio: $1.25M–$2.25M.
- **Fat FIRE:** Abundant lifestyle without financial compromises ($120k–$250k+/year). Portfolio: $3M–$7M+.
- **Coast FIRE:** Having saved enough early so that compound growth alone reaches your retirement target by age 60–65 without any additional contributions. You only need to work enough to cover daily living expenses.
- **Barista FIRE:** Partial financial independence supplemented by part-time, low-stress employment (often for health insurance benefits).

## 5. Coast FIRE Mathematical Formula

$$\text{Coast FIRE Target} = \frac{\text{Traditional FIRE Target}}{(1 + r)^t}$$

Where:
- $r$ = Real expected annual rate of return (typically 5% to 7% inflation-adjusted).
- $t$ = Number of years until traditional retirement age.

*Worked Example:*
- Desired FIRE target at age 65: **$1,500,000**
- Current age: **30** ($t = 35$ years)
- Real return: **6%** ($r = 0.06$)
- $\text{Coast FIRE Target at 30} = \frac{\$1,500,000}{(1 + 0.06)^{35}} = \frac{\$1,500,000}{7.686} = \mathbf{\$195,157}$.
*(Once a 30-year-old accumulates $195k in an index fund, they never need to contribute another dollar to retirement.)*

## 7. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you withdraw retirement funds before age 59½ without penalties?
Yes. Techniques include:
1. **Roth IRA Conversion Ladder:** Convert Traditional IRA/401(k) to Roth IRA, wait a 5-year seasoning period, then withdraw principal penalty-free.
2. **Rule 72(t) / SEPP:** Substantially Equal Periodic Payments based on life expectancy.
3. **Taxable Brokerage Accounts:** No age limits or early withdrawal penalties on capital gains and dividends.

### What is the biggest risk to early retirement?
Sequence of Returns Risk (SRR) — the danger of experiencing a major market downturn during the first 3 to 5 years of withdrawals. To mitigate SRR, retirees maintain a 1 to 2 year cash buffer or bond tent.

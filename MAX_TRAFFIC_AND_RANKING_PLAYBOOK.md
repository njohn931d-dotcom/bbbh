## 3. Pillar 2: Parasite SEO on GitHub Pages (DA 99)

GitHub Pages (`username.github.io`) inherits domain authority from `github.io` (DA 99 / Moz 96).

### 3.1 Overcoming the Doorway Page Trap (Programmatic Distinctness)
Earlier parasite SEO setups generated 40 near-identical pages from a single template (same copy, same "$100 / $500" table, differing only in the title keyword). In 2025–2026, search engine "scaled content abuse" algorithms collapse duplicate pages into a single ranking or issue a manual action.

To win at parasite SEO without penalty risk, **every single page must be genuinely distinct**:
- **Distinct Formula:** Mathematical definition and explanation tailored specifically to the problem (e.g., standard amortization for mortgages, annualized 12-month compounding for subscriptions, 2,080-hour divisor for salaries).
- **Calculated Example Tables:** Concrete numbers with explicit assumptions (e.g., 20-year vs 30-year mortgage rates; $10 vs $50 monthly subscription ladders).
- **Unique FAQs with Schema Markup:** 2 to 3 real questions per page encoded in JSON-LD `FAQPage` schema. This directly captures Google's **People Also Ask (PAA)** rich snippet boxes.
- **Client-Side Interactivity:** Real vanilla JS calculators that compute instantly on user input, creating high dwell times and near-zero bounce rates.

## 5. Pillar 4: Generative Engine Optimization (GEO - AI Search Engines)

Modern search traffic increasingly originates from AI answer engines (ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews). GEO ensures our repository and site are cited as the authoritative source:

1. **`llms.txt` and `ai.txt`:**
   - Standardized plain-text discovery files published at `/llms.txt` and `/ai.txt` in the site root.
   - Summarizes every single tool, its mathematical formula, and direct URL.
   - LLM crawlers read this index directly to answer user queries with linked citations.
2. **Direct-Answer Snippets:**
   - The first two sentences of every calculator and guide state the direct numerical answer and formula clearly (e.g. *"At a $70,000 annual salary, your hourly wage is $33.65 based on 2,080 working hours per year."*).
   - AI models ingest this exact phrasing for direct quotations.
3. **Structured JSON-LD Entity Graphs:**
   - Every page declares `@type: "WebApplication"`, `@type: "TechArticle"`, `@type: "FAQPage"`, and `@type: "BreadcrumbList"`.
   - Links explicitly to `codeRepository: "https://github.com/njohn931d-dotcom/bbbh"` to establish open-source authenticity and E-E-A-T.
4. **AI Bot Access in `robots.txt`:**
   - Explicitly permits `GPTBot`, `ChatGPT-User`, `PerplexityBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `anthropic-ai`, `CCBot`, `Bytespider`, and `Google-Extended`.
